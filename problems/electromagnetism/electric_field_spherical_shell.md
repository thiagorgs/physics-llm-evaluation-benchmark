# Electric Field of a Uniformly Charged Spherical Shell

## Topic

Electromagnetism

## Difficulty

Undergraduate

## Problem Statement

Consider a thin spherical shell of radius $R$ carrying a total charge $Q$ uniformly distributed over its surface.

Using Gauss's law, determine the electric field $\mathbf{E}(r)$ at a distance $r$ from the center of the shell for the two regions:

1. $r < R$
2. $r > R$

Assume electrostatic equilibrium and spherical symmetry.

## Expected Reasoning Skills

- Application of Gauss's law.
- Identification of the appropriate Gaussian surface.
- Use of spherical symmetry.
- Distinction between the regions inside and outside the charged shell.
- Physical interpretation of the electric field behavior.

## Target Result

For $r < R$,

$$
\mathbf{E}(r) = 0.
$$

For $r > R$,

$$
\mathbf{E}(r) =
\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}
\frac{Q}{r^2}\hat{\mathbf{r}}.
$$
