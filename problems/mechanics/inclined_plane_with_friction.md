# Inclined Plane with Friction and an External Force

## Topic

Classical Mechanics

## Difficulty

Undergraduate

## Problem Statement

A block of mass $m$ moves upward along an inclined plane that makes an angle $\theta$ with the horizontal. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the block and the plane is $\mu_k$.

A constant force $F$ is applied parallel to the plane, pointing upward along the incline.

Assuming the block is moving upward along the plane, derive an expression for its acceleration.

## Expected Reasoning Skills

- Decomposition of gravitational force into components parallel and perpendicular to the incline.
- Correct identification of the normal force.
- Correct direction of kinetic friction.
- Application of Newton's second law along the incline.
- Interpretation of limiting cases.

## Target Result

$$
a = \frac{F}{m} - g\sin\theta - \mu_k g\cos\theta.
$$
