# Spin-1/2 Particle in a Magnetic Field

## Topic

Quantum Mechanics

## Difficulty

Undergraduate / Early Graduate

## Problem Statement

Consider a spin-1/2 particle in a uniform magnetic field applied along the \(z\)-axis. The Hamiltonian is

\[
H = -\gamma B S_z,
\]

where \(\gamma\) is the gyromagnetic ratio, \(B\) is the magnetic field magnitude, and

\[
S_z = \frac{\hbar}{2}\sigma_z.
\]

The particle is initially prepared in the state

\[
|\psi(0)\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(|+\rangle_z + |-\rangle_z\right),
\]

where \(|+\rangle_z\) and \(|-\rangle_z\) are eigenstates of \(S_z\).

1. Find the time-evolved state \(|\psi(t)\rangle\).
2. Compute the expectation value \(\langle S_x(t)\rangle\).
3. Interpret the physical meaning of the result.

## Expected Reasoning Skills

- Identification of energy eigenstates.
- Time evolution under a time-independent Hamiltonian.
- Use of spin operators and Pauli matrices.
- Calculation of expectation values.
- Physical interpretation of spin precession.

## Target Result

\[
\langle S_x(t)\rangle = \frac{\hbar}{2}\cos(\gamma Bt).
\]
