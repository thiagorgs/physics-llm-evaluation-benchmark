# Two-Level System and Partition Function

## Topic

Statistical Physics

## Difficulty

Undergraduate / Early Graduate

## Problem Statement

Consider a system with two possible energy levels:

$$
E_0 = 0,
$$

and

$$
E_1 = \epsilon,
$$

where $\epsilon > 0$. The system is in thermal equilibrium with a heat bath at temperature $T$.

1. Compute the canonical partition function $Z$.
2. Find the average energy $\langle E \rangle$.
3. Analyze the limits $T \rightarrow 0$ and $T \rightarrow \infty$.
4. Interpret the physical meaning of these limits.

## Expected Reasoning Skills

- Use of the canonical ensemble.
- Construction of the partition function.
- Calculation of thermal averages.
- Interpretation of low-temperature and high-temperature limits.
- Connection between mathematical expressions and physical behavior.

## Target Result

$$
Z = 1 + e^{-\beta \epsilon}
$$

and

$$
\langle E \rangle =
\frac{\epsilon e^{-\beta \epsilon}}{1 + e^{-\beta \epsilon}},
$$

where

$$
\beta = \frac{1}{k_B T}.
$$
